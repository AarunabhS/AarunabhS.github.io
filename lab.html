The details matter

A result is only as useful
as its context.

Every project carries its data source, the method behind it, and the limits of what it can tell you.

01 Recorded benchmarks +

Fraud, churn, sentiment and click-through ranking use exported results from reviewed repository revisions. Changing a control explores those results. Saved model examples keep their original inputs and recorded scores.

02 Illustrative datasets +

Booking patterns use 1,000 deterministic synthetic records. Talent Radar uses fictional companies and jobs. Both are labelled in the workspace so you can explore the approach with the right context.

03 Original experiences +

Watch Atlas and Country Memory Map open the original public applications on request. PlayerPulse uses a saved player archive; the comparison shows missing coverage rather than filling gaps with invented values.
